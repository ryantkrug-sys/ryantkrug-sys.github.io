SAT Formula Sheet — the highest-priority formulas

From Ryan Krug, SAT prep instructor (Math with Ryan), Tampa, FL. The SAT gives you a short reference sheet — these are the formulas it leaves off that are worth knowing cold.

Lines & Algebra Essentials

  • Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
  • Slope-intercept: y = mx + b (b = y-intercept)
  • Standard form: Ax + By = C, slope = −A/B
  • Midpoint: ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
  • Distance: √((x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²)
  • Parallel lines: same slope
  • Perpendicular lines: slopes multiply to −1
  • Systems: no solution = parallel (same slope, different intercept); infinitely many = same line
  • Inequalities: flip the sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative

Quadratics

  • Quadratic formula: x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a
  • Discriminant: b² − 4ac > 0 → 2 real solutions; = 0 → 1; < 0 → none
  • Vertex form: y = a(x − h)² + k, vertex (h, k)
  • Vertex x-coordinate: x = −b / 2a
  • Factored form: y = a(x − r)(x − s), roots r and s
  • Difference of squares: a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b)
  • Perfect squares: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²; (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
  • Sum of the roots: −b/a  ·  Product: c/a

Exponents, Radicals & Functions

  • Product: xᵃ · xᵇ = xᵃ⁺ᵇ
  • Quotient: xᵃ / xᵇ = xᵃ⁻ᵇ
  • Power of a power: (xᵃ)ᵇ = xᵃᵇ
  • Negative exponent: x⁻ᵃ = 1 / xᵃ
  • Fractional exponent: x^(1/n) = ⁿ√x; √x = x^(1/2)
  • Radical product: √a · √b = √(ab)
  • Function shifts: f(x) + k shifts up k; f(x − h) shifts right h
  • Exponential growth/decay: y = a(1 + r)ᵗ / y = a(1 − r)ᵗ

Problem Solving & Data

  • Percent change: (new − old) / old × 100%
  • Percent of a number: part = percent × whole
  • Mean: sum of values / number of values
  • Median: middle value when ordered (average the two middle values if even count)
  • Probability: favorable outcomes / total outcomes
  • Proportion: a/b = c/d → cross-multiply: ad = bc
  • Rate: distance = rate × time; use unit conversions to cancel units
  • Simple interest: I = Prt (rarely tested, but cheap to know)

Geometry

  • Circle equation: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r², center (h, k)
  • Circle: area = πr²; circumference = 2πr
  • Arc length: (θ/360°) × 2πr  ·  Sector area: (θ/360°) × πr²
  • Triangle area: ½ × base × height
  • Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c²
  • 45-45-90 triangle: sides x, x, x√2
  • 30-60-90 triangle: sides x, x√3, 2x
  • Similar triangles: corresponding sides are proportional
  • Angles: triangle angles sum to 180°; straight line = 180°; vertical angles are equal
  • Rectangle: area = length × width; Volume of a box: l × w × h

Trigonometry

  • SOH-CAH-TOA: sin = opp/hyp; cos = adj/hyp; tan = opp/adj
  • Pythagorean identity: sin²x + cos²x = 1
  • Complementary angles: sin(x°) = cos((90 − x)°)
  • tan: tan(x) = sin(x) / cos(x)
  • Degrees ↔ radians: 180° = π radians; multiply degrees by π/180
  • Unit circle anchors: sin 30° = ½; cos 30° = √3/2; sin 45° = √2/2
